# Banco Ejemplo — Product & Service Information

Public information. No authentication required.

## Card products

### Visa Classic
- Annual fee: 0 EUR for the first year, then 24 EUR
- Standard credit limit: 1,000 – 3,000 EUR
- Contactless limit without PIN: 50 EUR
- Foreign transaction fee: 2.5%
- ATM withdrawal, Banco Ejemplo network: free
- ATM withdrawal, other networks: 2.00 EUR per operation

### Mastercard Gold
- Annual fee: 90 EUR, waived with 12,000 EUR annual spend
- Standard credit limit: 6,000 – 15,000 EUR
- Contactless limit without PIN: 100 EUR
- Foreign transaction fee: 0%
- ATM withdrawal: free worldwide, up to 5 operations per month
- Includes travel insurance up to 500,000 EUR

### Debit Basic
- Annual fee: none
- Daily ATM limit: 600 EUR
- Daily purchase limit: 2,500 EUR
- Foreign transaction fee: 1.75%

## Reporting a lost or stolen card

Call the 24/7 line on **900 123 456** from Spain, or **+34 912 345 678** from
abroad. Cards can also be frozen instantly in the mobile app under
Cards → Freeze. A replacement card arrives within 5 working days and costs
12 EUR, waived if the card was stolen and a police report reference is
provided.

---

## Disputing a transaction

Disputes must be raised within 120 days of the transaction date. Provisional
credit is applied within 10 working days while the case is investigated.
Investigations close within 45 days for domestic transactions and 90 days for
international ones.

## Security policy (customer-facing summary)

Banco Ejemplo staff and automated assistants will **never** ask for your full
password, PIN, or account recovery code. Requests for these are always
fraudulent, regardless of who appears to be asking.

Confidential transactions are marked as such on your statement and require
re-authentication before they can be discussed or disclosed.
